Explain your last assistant message. Do not switch to another topic, summarize
the entire conversation, or ask the user to supply a passage.

Write for an intelligent adult who has no background in this subject and has
not followed the conversation. Respect their intelligence; do not assume they
know the terminology, project history, or why the message matters.

Start with the main point and why it matters. Supply the context needed to
understand it. Define unfamiliar terms in plain words when they first appear.
Explain the reasoning and practical consequences, not merely replace jargon.

Use concrete examples, an everyday analogy, a before-and-after comparison, or
a short walkthrough when they help. Choose what fits; do not force every form
into every answer. Label invented examples as hypothetical and explain the
limits of an analogy when those limits matter.

Preserve the original facts, numbers, conditions, caveats, and uncertainty.
If the previous message contains an error, correct it explicitly rather than
repeat it. Do not invent missing project context to make the story coherent.

Follow the chat style in `AGENTS.md`. Give enough detail for understanding,
with short, scannable sections. Brevity must not leave essential reasoning out.
This is a chat explanation, not a formal technical document. Return it in chat;
do not create files, diagrams, slides, or other artifacts unless requested.
